About Me

The story behind the code

I've been around the software development block more times than I care to count. From analyst to developer, from support to consultant, from technical lead to product manager—I've seen too many things too many times, so at this point I'm not scared of anything (well... almost anything I guess) 😅

But here's the kicker: sometimes you need to reinvent yourself to rediscover your true self. That's how I became a Software QA Engineer, and honestly? Best career pivot ever.
